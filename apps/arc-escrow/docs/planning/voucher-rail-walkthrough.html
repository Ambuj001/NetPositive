Voucher Rail Walkthrough

A crypto holder who wants to shop gets a gift voucher. A card holder who wants crypto buys that voucher on her best reward route and keeps 100% of her card rewards. A verification layer checks the voucher is live before the escrow releases the USDT. No rupees move between strangers.

5Verification layer

The codes are checked live before Rohan sees them

Priya pastes the codes into the extension. They go encrypted to our verification layer, which checks balance and status on the issuer's balance-check page. For vouchers above ₹10,000, or brands like jewellers (Kalyan, Tanishq), it checks on the merchant's own portal. Only a pass moves the trade forward.

Tech
  • Codes encrypted on Priya's device to the verifier key; deleted after the trade
  • Verifier queries the issuer or merchant balance check, then signs: order ID, amount, status, time
  • Escrow contract accepts only a signed pass. Stretch: a zkTLS proof of the balance page instead of trusting our signature

6Rohan

Rohan gets the codes, the escrow pays Priya

The codes open only for Rohan. The verifier re-checks the balance at that moment, because Priya also has the codes. Then the contract sends 111.11 USDT to Priya and splits the 1.11 USDT fee. Rohan shops on Saturday.

Tech
  • Codes re-encrypted to Rohan's key; second verifier check right before release
  • Contract releases 111.11 USDT to Priya
  • Fee split (proposal): 0.56 USDT to the dispute reserve, 0.55 USDT platform revenue

7If something goes wrong

The 1% fee works like card network dispute money

Like Visa or Mastercard charging merchants to fund chargebacks, part of every 1% fee builds a reserve. If a voucher was spent before Rohan used it, the reserve pays him back up to a limit. Bigger trades can require the voucher provider to post a bond.

Tech
  • Reserve held in the contract, payouts need a verifier-signed dispute result
  • Per-person dispute and trade limits tied to World ID, not to wallets

8Any user

Chat explains every pick and every rejection

Users can ask why a card or route was chosen and why the others weren't. Answers come from the same numbers the engine used, with the source and the date each rule was checked.

Tech
  • AI reads the engine's ranked list and its rule citations; it doesn't do the maths itself
  • Sends only the purchase and card names, never the user's full portfolio

The tech stack, top to bottom

Browser extensionSeller view (merchant detection, voucher request, crypto card comparison) and buyer view (order feed, best card and route, guided purchase). Card portfolio stays on the device.
Rewards engine + chatCard catalogue for India (credit and debit, all major banks), Europe (your list) and crypto prepaid cards. Channel multipliers, caps and exclusions, each with source and date. Chat explains the ranking.
Order bookVoucher requests from sellers, matched to buyers by brand, amount and rate.
Verification layerChecks each code live with the issuer (GyFTR, Woohoo, Pine Labs) or the merchant's own portal for high values and jewellers. Signs pass or fail. Re-checks before release.
Escrow contractHolds USDT, releases only on a signed pass, splits the 1% fee into reserve and revenue, pays small disputes.
IdentityWorld ID: one real person per account, bans that stick.

Our working rules

  • Re-check the latest official terms for every card and channel before showing a number. Show the source and the date checked.
  • Never collect card numbers, CVV, PIN or OTP. Voucher codes are encrypted and deleted after the trade.
  • Rewards belong to the user's card. We only predict them; we never pay or promise them.
  • Hackathon: build only after kickoff, open-source repo, list where AI helped, demo video 2–4 minutes.

Must solve before this goes live

  • Voucher terms: Amazon Pay gift cards "cannot be resold, transferred for value or redeemed for cash". Check each brand's terms; the issuer can cancel vouchers or close accounts, and HDFC can withhold points.
  • Balance checks: automated lookups on issuer and merchant portals need their permission or an API. For the demo, use one issuer.
  • The voucher provider keeps a copy of the code. The re-check and reserve reduce this risk; they don't remove it.
  • Tax: crypto trades paid in kind may still need TDS under Section 194S. Confirm with a CA.
