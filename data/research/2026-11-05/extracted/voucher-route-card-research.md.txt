Rules the engine always applies:
- Per-transaction rounding (points per ₹150 or ₹200 are rounded down on each voucher).
- Remaining monthly cap, reserved while an order is open.
- Exclusion lists (InstaDeals vs accelerated; Magnus gift-card category; wallet loads).
- Points posting delay (SmartBuy: up to 90 working days) shown as "expected", not
"earned".
- Source link and "checked on" date shown with every number.
4. What this file deliberately leaves out
Ways of earning on vouchers where the issuer's terms say you shouldn't (merchant-code tricks,
splitting to dodge caps, routing through wallets) are not included. The engine only recommends
routes the bank officially offers, because users who rely on workarounds risk losing points or
having their card closed.
