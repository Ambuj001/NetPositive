# 20 crypto card programmes: category rewards and optimisation research

**Research date: 5 November 2026.** Currency symbols and cap units are preserved from sources. This is a global research shortlist for our payment-optimisation product, with particular attention to Europe. It is **not an independently measured ranking of the world's 20 largest or best cards**, and is not a claim that all 20 can be issued to Indian or European residents.

## Reading the rates correctly

- **R** means the card's tier/funding-dependent general eligible-purchase rate, detailed in that entry. It is not a separate category bonus.
- **R†** means the general rate is a candidate for that category, subject to the actual merchant's MCC and transaction exclusions. Category-specific eligibility has not been established for every merchant. The engine must check before recommending.
- **NV** means not verified from the current accessible evidence. It does not mean 0%, unlimited, free, or available.
- **Excluded** means an explicit exclusion was found for the specified transaction or MCC. Acceptance and rewards eligibility are different.
- **Published** means the official page states the rule. It does not mean an application, account-specific offer, or purchase was tested.
- **Provisional** means an important current schedule, eligibility condition, or source could not be resolved. Block guaranteed-rate recommendations until resolved.
- Percentages describe value at award/calculation, not guaranteed future cash value. Token appreciation is not included. Interest on an idle balance is not card cashback.

**Geography rule:** a card accepted in India is not necessarily a card issuable to an Indian resident. US-only credit cards must not be recommended as application options for Indian/European residents without independently establishing eligibility. EEA, UK, Switzerland and other European markets require separate records. Country-level application eligibility was not exhaustively verified for this report; no India application approval is asserted.

**Source rule:** linked official product/help/legal pages are the evidence for the adjacent statements. Search-index retrieval was used where direct pages were inaccessible; those limitations are identified. No signed-in card offers, issuer API, complete circular archive or individual user's rates were accessed. This is a dated research snapshot, not a continuously refreshed production database.

## Rules required for our product

### Card and programme identity

Store issuer/programme, exact product, country of residence, issuing entity, network, funding mode, reward currency, subscription/holding tier, status, source URL, effective date and last-checked timestamp. Regional credit and prepaid variants must not share a reward record merely because the brand matches.

### Category and route eligibility

Store actual MCC, merchant identifiers, online/in-store channel, mobile-wallet requirement, permitted funding asset, gift-card policy and exclusions. Merchant brand names alone are insufficient. A gift-card intermediary can change the MCC and eligibility; do not inherit the end merchant's reward category. Where an issuer bans gift-card rewards, do not route a user there to chase a multiplier.

### Stacking and cap allocation

Keep base reward, incremental bonus, subscription rebate, referral bonus, welcome offer and speculative points in separate fields. Add them only when programme terms explicitly permit it. Reserve estimated cap usage against simultaneous purchases; update using settlement, refunds and clawbacks. A nominal “20% subscription” rate must use its service-specific payment cap and renewal period.

### Net value

```text
net benefit = verified immediate discount
            + realistically redeemable reward value
            + permitted offer rebate
            - incremental card/payment/FX/conversion fees
            - attributable membership and financing costs
            - redemption costs and expected unused-credit loss
```

Use a separate scenario for capital opportunity cost, staking exposure, collateral requirements and token-price sensitivity. Do not subtract the entire annual fee for every purchase. Show marginal value for an already-owned card and annual value for a new-card decision separately.

### Recommendation gates

A source with no current rate is not a zero-rate card; it is an unresolved candidate. An expired offer cannot win the route ranking. If a material schedule is missing, request in-app evidence or provide a clearly provisional comparison. Do not present this research file as production-authoritative executable rules.
